What's Your Triadic Motivational Pattern?

The Enneagram is traditionally used to identify a person's Primary motivation, one of 9 possible types. Discovering this helps to guide that person's journey toward healthier wellbeing. (as shown by the Enneagram clockface arrows)

The TMEG framework works in parallel, finding the top-scoring type in each of the three cleverness regions of Head-Thinking, Heart-Feeling and Gut/Body-Instinct. This nurture-derived Triadic motivational pattern then shows the bridge to our nature-based personality preferences, giving a fuller picture of a person.

TMEG has not been scientifically validated, and there are no large datasets behind it. Instead, it is twenty-five years of independent research, offered in the hope that someone finds it useful and worth exploring further.

Acknowledgements

The TMEG questionnaire is an Enneagram test that focuses on gauging a person’s possible PRIMARY MOTIVATION and their TRIADIC (head-heart-gut) PATTERN. It uses a Virtues approach to support the positive arrow lines of improvement that make the Enneagram so helpful.

To ensure descriptor word integrity and uniqueness, as shown in the TMEG results, I accessed synonym functionality using www.Thesaurus.com and www.PowerThesaurus.org.

About

Welcome to my Enneagram and Triadics research work. I hope this work is helpful to bring insight and understanding about what makes us human.

My Bio: I was first introduced to Myers-Briggs and other profiling tools in the late 80’s, but my interest in personality and motivational psychology really began to take off in the early 2000’s after I had attended an ‘Introduction to the Enneagram’ course through a local church. I became a qualified MBTI practitioner and trainer in 2016 and a member of the IEA (International Enneagram Association) in 2017. I started my working life as a primary school teacher but within a few years changed to computer systems work. My aim is to help encourage the use of the Enneagram and other tools like MBTI, as mechanisms that can enhance self-awareness and assist people with self-actualising their life journeys.

This work has been years in the making, with all sorts of iterations and improvements applied over time. Such work cannot be undertaken without the tremendous input and help I have received from those in my life who have been willing to share their time, thoughts and allow a lot of revisiting my TMEG test to help ensure as much clarity and accuracy as possible in interpreting the results. Therefore my sincerest thanks, throughout these years, to all these friends, including various work colleagues, and especially to my kind and supportive, better half.
